Both include the same features: your listing in the discovery feed, appearance in search results sorted by proximity, portfolio, reviews, and bookings. Both renew automatically until canceled.

Cutlas reserves the right to modify pricing with 30 days' notice.

All new barbers receive a 30-day free trial, starting when your listing is created. Your listing is visible to customers once your trial subscription is in place; if you skip checkout, it stays hidden until you start the trial from the Subscription screen. The trial is offered once per listing: canceling and re-subscribing does not restart it, and subscribing with less than 48 hours left in the trial begins billing immediately.

  • Subscriptions are billed on a recurring monthly or annual cycle, per your selected option.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. By subscribing, you authorize recurring charges.
  • All prices are in U.S. dollars.

  • Cutlas does not store your card details — all processing is handled by Stripe.

4. LISTING ACTIVATION

Your listing is active while your subscription is in trial, active, or in the failed-payment grace period. If your subscription lapses, your listing is deactivated and hidden from customers. Reactivating does not grant a new trial.

5. CANCELLATION

  • Cancel at any time via the Subscription screen ("Manage billing").
  • Your listing remains active until the end of the current trial or billing period.
  • Cancellation does not entitle you to a refund for the current period.

Subscription fees are non-refundable. No refunds for partial billing periods. Contact cutlased@gmail.com within 7 days if you believe you were charged in error.

Switch between monthly and annual billing at any time via the billing portal. Timing and any proration follow our payment processor's standard handling and are shown before you confirm.

A Boost gives your listing priority placement for 7 days. It is a one-time $4.00 purchase (or a free referral credit) — not a subscription, no auto-renewal.
