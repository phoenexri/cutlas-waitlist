Delete Your Cutlas Account

You can permanently delete your Cutlas account and all associated data at any time.

OPTION 1 — IN THE APP (INSTANT)

  • Open Cutlas and sign in.
  • Go to the Account tab.
  • Tap Delete account, confirm, and enter your password.

OPTION 2 — BY EMAIL

Email cutlased@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We complete emailed requests within 30 days.

WHAT GETS DELETED

  • Your account and login credentials.
  • Your profile (name, phone, verification status).
  • If you are a barber: your listing, portfolio photos, and reviews received; any active subscription is canceled immediately.
  • If you are a customer: your reviews, bookings, and saved barbers.

WHAT WE RETAIN

Payment records required for tax and fraud-prevention purposes are retained by our payment processor (Stripe) per their retention policy. We do not retain your profile data after deletion.
