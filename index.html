How It Works

Customers

#1

Browse barbers near you. Real portfolios of the closest chairs.

#2

Verify your email once after you log in to unlock the vast catalog of independent barbers. It keeps both sides safe.

#3

Reserve your slot for $1. Pick a time, pay a $1 reservation fee, and you're booked. The cut itself is paid to your barber directly.

Barbers

#1

Create your listing free for 30 days. Card required to start the trial: Premium is $9/mo or $79/yr after.

#2

Upload your work. Post photos of your cuts, fades, and styles. Your portfolio is your storefront, and customers book what they can see.

#3

Fill your chair. Bookings come to you; the service payment stays 100% yours, off-platform.
